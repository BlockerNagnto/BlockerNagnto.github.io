Template Editor: Loadout

This tool is designed to help you quickly construct and visualize the {{Loadout}} template for The Corebound Wiki. Select a tier, pick your items from the right panel to fill the visual grid, and the corresponding wikitext will be automatically generated below. Dynamic tech slots are calculated based on your hull tier, node bonuses, and Nexus bonus, matching Module:HullLayout.
